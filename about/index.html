Carpets, upholstery, tile and grout, windows, bond cleans, builders cleans, commercial contracts and flood drying.

Who we are

Ultraclean Swan Hill is a locally owned cleaning business operating from 529 Lakeside Drive, Lake Boga, about 17 km south of Swan Hill. We are not a franchise and not a booking platform: when you call 0417 327 173 you speak to the person who will quote the job and turn up to do it.

The business started with carpet steam cleaning for homes and rentals around Swan Hill and Lake Boga. Customers kept asking whether we could do the lounge, the tiles, the windows and the whole house before an inspection — so the service list grew to nine services, and the run grew to twenty towns from Sea Lake to Balranald.

What we do

  • Carpet Cleaning — Hot-water extraction that lifts dirt, pet odour and traffic lanes out of the fibre — not just the surface.
  • Upholstery & Rug Cleaning — Lounges, dining chairs, mattresses and wool or synthetic rugs cleaned in place or picked up and returned.
  • Tile & Grout Cleaning — High-pressure turbo cleaning that brings grout lines back to their original colour, then seals them.
  • Window Cleaning — Streak-free inside and out for homes, shopfronts and offices — frames, tracks and screens included.
  • Bond / End of Lease Cleaning — Full vacate clean to your agent's checklist, with carpets included, so your bond comes back in full.
  • Commercial & Builders Cleaning — Regular contract cleaning for offices, shops and clinics, plus post-construction builders cleans.
  • Flood & Water Damage — Fast water extraction, carpet drying and sanitising after floods, burst pipes and overflows.

Equipment that does the job properly

Results come down to gear as much as effort. Ultraclean runs truck-mounted hot-water extraction for carpets and upholstery (hotter water, stronger vacuum recovery, faster drying than portable units), high-pressure turbo tools for tile and grout, water-fed poles for second-storey glass, and commercial air movers and dehumidifiers for water damage. Everything travels on the truck, so one visit covers the whole job.

Safe products, rural-ready

Many properties around Swan Hill, Kerang and the NSW river towns are on septic systems and tank water. We use low-residue, biodegradable products that are safe for kids, pets and septic tanks, and we rinse thoroughly so nothing sticky is left behind to attract dirt.

Insured, checked, accountable

Ultraclean carries public liability insurance and our operators are police checked. Property managers, builders and commercial clients can request certificates. If a job is not right, you call the same number and we come back and fix it.

Where we work

Victoria: Swan Hill, Lake Boga, Kerang, Nyah, Piangil, Wood Wood, Beverford, Sea Lake, Lalbert, Ultima, Quambatook, Cohuna, Manangatang. New South Wales: Murray Downs, Koraleigh, Tooleybuc, Moulamein, Balranald, Barham, Kyalite. See the service areas page for details on each town. There is no travel surcharge inside the service area.
