Window cleaning Swan Hill homes and businesses

Dust storms, irrigation spray and hard bore water leave a film on glass that ordinary cleaning just smears around. Ultraclean window cleaning in Swan Hill uses purified water and professional squeegees for a streak-free finish, and a water-fed pole for second-storey and hard-to-reach glass so nobody has to climb a ladder over your garden.

What is included

  • Inside and outside of every pane
  • Frames and sills wiped down
  • Tracks vacuumed and cleaned
  • Flyscreens removed, washed and refitted
  • Skylights, glass doors and pool fencing on request
  • Cobwebs cleared from around the windows

Questions

Window Cleaning FAQ

Do you clean two-storey windows?
Yes, using a water-fed pole from the ground. It reaches most second-storey glass safely without ladders.
How often should shop windows be cleaned?
Most Swan Hill shopfronts look their best on a fortnightly clean; quieter side-street offices are fine monthly.
Do you remove hard water spots?
Light mineral spotting comes off with the standard clean. Heavily etched glass may need a restoration treatment, which we can quote separately.
Do you travel to Balranald and Barham for window cleaning?
We do — both are within our service area.
