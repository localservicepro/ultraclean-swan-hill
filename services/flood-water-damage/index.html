Flood carpet drying Swan Hill — what to do in the first 48 hours

Wet carpet does not dry on its own in a closed house. Within 48 hours the underlay starts to smell, and within a few days mould can take hold in the carpet, the underlay and the bottom of the plasterboard. Ultraclean flood and water damage drying in Swan Hill starts with extracting as much water as possible with a truck-mounted machine, then lifting the carpet to dry the underlay directly with air movers and a dehumidifier. Most rooms are dry within two to three days, and the carpet is relaid, cleaned and sanitised.

What we do

  • Emergency water extraction from carpet, underlay and hard floors
  • Carpet lifted and underlay dried or replaced
  • Commercial air movers and dehumidifiers installed and monitored
  • Moisture readings taken in carpet, subfloor and walls before we sign off
  • Antimicrobial treatment to stop mould and odour
  • Carpet relaid, steam cleaned and deodorised
  • Photos and a drying report for your insurer

Insurance claims

If the water came from a burst pipe, hot water service, appliance or storm, your home or landlord insurance will usually cover the drying and cleaning. We document the moisture readings, the equipment used and the drying time so your claim goes through without back-and-forth. You can call us before the assessor has been — starting the drying early is what the insurer wants too.

River flooding along the Murray

When the Murray or the Little Murray rises, homes in Swan Hill, Murray Downs, Lake Boga, Nyah and Tooleybuc can take water through the floor. River water is category 3 (contaminated), which means the carpet and underlay generally need to be removed and the subfloor sanitised rather than dried in place. We can help with the strip-out and the sanitising clean so the house is safe before the rebuild starts.

Flood & Water Damage FAQ

Can you dry out flooded carpets in Swan Hill and Lake Boga?
Yes. We extract the water, lift the carpet, dry the underlay with air movers and dehumidifiers, then relay and clean the carpet. Most rooms are dry in two to three days.
How fast can you get here?
Call 0417 327 173. For water damage in Swan Hill, Lake Boga and Kerang we aim to be on site the same day, because every hour counts.
Can the carpet be saved?
Clean water from a pipe or appliance — usually yes, if we get to it within a day or two. River or sewage water — the carpet and underlay normally have to go.
Do you work with insurance companies?
We do. We provide moisture readings, photos and a drying report and can talk directly with your assessor.
